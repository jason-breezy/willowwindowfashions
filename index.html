Signature feature

Plantation shutters, expertly installed

Shutters are the most luxurious upgrade you can give a window, and installing them right is a true craft. Few installers on the Shore specialize in them. Ivan has been building and fitting shutters since his Levolor days, and every set is custom made and hung to your exact openings.

  • Hardwood and moisture-resistant composite, ideal for shore homes, kitchens and baths
  • Classic and wide louver sizes, from traditional to modern
  • Hidden tilt options for a clean, uninterrupted look
  • Made to fit arches, bay windows, sliders and specialty shapes
  • A lasting upgrade that adds real value to your home

Our story

A family business, run by a husband and wife

Willow Window Fashions is family-owned. When you call, you talk to the people whose name is on the door.

Ivan

Founder · In the trade since 1978

Ivan started on the factory floor at Levolor and has spent over 45 years in blinds, shades and shutters. He knows how they are built, not just how they hang, and he has seen every fabric, mechanism and tricky window, and he'll measure, recommend and install it the right way.

Maryann

Runs the shop · Co-owner

Maryann keeps the shop running smoothly, from orders and scheduling to making sure every customer is looked after from first call to final install.

How Willow came to be

  • 1978 · Learning the trade at LevolorIvan starts on the packing line at Levolor's Hoboken, NJ plant, one of the most famous names in blinds. He works his way up to floor supervisor, then electronic technician, learning how window coverings are built from the inside out.
  • 1988 · Willow Window Fashions is bornWith a decade of factory know-how behind him, Ivan strikes out on his own and founds Willow Window Fashions.
  • 2000 · The Bayville showroom opensIvan and Maryann open the doors in Bayville, NJ, now at Roberts Plaza on Route 9, and they're still here today.
  • Today · Still family-runMore than 35 years in, Willow is still a husband-and-wife shop: Ivan's craftsmanship and Maryann's care for every customer, under one roof.

Questions

Good to know

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. We visit your home, show samples, measure and give you a quote at no charge or obligation.

How long does it take to get my window coverings?

Most custom orders arrive in roughly 2–4 weeks depending on the product. We'll give you a firm timeline with your quote.

Do you install everything?

Yes. Our team handles professional installation and shows you how to operate everything.

Are cordless and motorized options available?

Nearly all of our products are available cordless, and many can be motorized and connected to your smart home.

Which areas do you serve?

We're based in Bayville and serve Ocean County, Monmouth County and the Jersey Shore. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.

Do you offer warranties?

Our products are backed by manufacturer warranties, and we stand behind our installation. Ask us for details.
