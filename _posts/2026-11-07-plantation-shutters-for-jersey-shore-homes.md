If you live near the water in Ocean or Monmouth County, your windows deal with a lot: strong sun off the bay, salty air, summer humidity and, if you rent your home out, a steady stream of guests. Plantation shutters handle all of it better than almost any other window treatment.

## They're built for humidity

Fabric shades and some wood products can warp, fade or hold moisture over time. Moisture-resistant composite shutters are made for exactly these conditions, which makes them a great fit for bathrooms, kitchens and homes a few blocks from the beach.

## Light control without losing the view

Tilt the louvers open for soft, filtered light, angle them for privacy while still seeing out, or close them fully to block the afternoon glare. One window treatment does the work of sheers, blinds and blackout shades.

## They add real value to your home

Because shutters are custom built and mounted to the window frame, they become part of the house. Buyers and renters notice them, and they're one of the few window treatments that can add to a home's appeal at resale.

## Easy to live with

There are no cords to tangle, which makes shutters a safe choice for families with young children and pets. Cleaning is a quick wipe of the louvers, and a well-made set lasts for decades.

## Installation is where it counts

Shutters are only as good as their fit. Windows are rarely perfectly square, and arches, bay windows and sliders all need special attention. That's why the measuring and installation matter as much as the product itself, and why fewer installers in the area specialize in them.
